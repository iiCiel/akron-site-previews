A hometown bakery, the way it used to be.

Vincent's Bakery has been baking for more than 60 years. The small, family-owned shop in a Bailey Rd strip plaza is known for its Italian wedding cake and cassata cakes, plus cannoli, cream sticks, donuts, strudel and cookies. There's plenty of parking but no seating, so grab a box to go. Open Wednesday through Saturday, 8am to 4pm.
