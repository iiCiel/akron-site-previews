What a cut takes

Time for each service, as listed on Heather's online booking page.

  • 30minutes

    Basic haircut / fade

    Clipper fade with the top trimmed. Includes a shampoo, product if you like, and a warm-lather straight razor neck shave.

  • 45minutes

    Men's haircut & beard trim

    Haircut with the beard trimmed to your length and lined up with the trimmers.

  • 40minutes

    Straight razor haircut

    As short as it goes on the sides with a straight razor. Length on top is up to you.

  • 25minutes

    Buzz cut

    Same length all over.

  • 20minutes

    Beard trim

    Clippers only, no razor.

  • 30minutes

    Retirees

    A 30-minute slot listed for retirees on Heather's page.

Services and times are from Heather's booking page and may change. Prices are listed there too, and her page says cash or check only. To see Brian, call the shop at (330) 607-2836.
